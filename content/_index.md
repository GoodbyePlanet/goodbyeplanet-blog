Hi there, I'm [GoodbyePlanet](https://github.com/GoodbyePlanet).

This blog site is intended to be a place where I will occasionally write about things that I've learned
during my work as a Software Developer.

I will strive to write blogs that are easy to understand and that get to the point. Most are a short read,
a few minutes at most. When a topic earns a proper deep dive I let it run longer, and every post shows its
reading time so you know what you are in for before you start.
My goal is to share practical knowledge, real-world experiences, and lessons learned along the way.
If something here helps you understand a concept faster or think about a problem differently,
then this blog has done its job.

All **Sketchnoting** images are generated with the *Gemini 3* model.
